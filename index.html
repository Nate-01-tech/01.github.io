Is this course suitable for complete beginners?
Yes. Module 1 is dedicated entirely to the absolute basics of Forex. However, the course quickly scales up to advanced Smart Money concepts.
Do I need a lot of capital to start trading?
No. We highly recommend starting on a demo account. Once you are consistent, we guide you on how to apply for prop firm funding so you don't have to risk your own capital.
What are ICT concepts?
ICT (Inner Circle Trader) concepts focus on reading price action based on liquidity and how algorithms deliver price, rather than relying on retail indicators like RSI or MACD.
